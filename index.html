Getting Started

  1. 1 Open Pace Buddy on your iPhone. There is no sign-up — the app uses your own iCloud account, so there is nothing to create and no password to remember.
  2. 2 Tap New Plan and pick a goal — a race like a 10k or a half marathon, or a habit like running three times a week. Pace Buddy builds a training plan aimed at that goal, with each day laid out as the next step toward it.
  3. 3 Your plan syncs to your Apple Watch automatically over the pairing. From then on the watch runs each workout on its own — you can leave the phone at home.
💡 You don't have to start with a goal. You can also bring in a plan you already have — see Importing a Plan below.
📅

Your Plan & Today

  1. 1 The Today screen answers one question: what is today's run, and why. It shows today's workout, your progress toward the goal, and the week around you.
  2. 2 Open the Plan to see every day, rest days included. You can add, move, or remove a workout, and edit the steps of any workout to suit you.
  3. 3 Skip a workout you can't do and it's marked skipped without breaking the plan. Today's framing stays honest about what's still ahead.
  4. 4 Export a plan as a .runplan file to share it or keep a copy, and open a .runplan someone sends you to load it straight in.
⌚

Running on the Watch

  1. 1 Open Pace Buddy on the watch and start today's workout. A short countdown gives you a moment to get going before the first step begins.
  2. 2 The run screen shows your pace, heart rate, distance, and the current step. The pace number turns green when you're on target, yellow when you're too slow, and red when you're too fast.
  3. 3 Pause and resume at any time — the workout clock stops while you're paused. Skip a step to move straight to the next one.
  4. 4 The run works fully offline. Leave your phone behind — the watch guides you the whole way and saves the run when you finish.
  5. 5 At the end, Pace Buddy asks how hard the run felt and saves it to Apple Health as a workout, with its route, so it counts toward your rings and your progress.
💡 Grant Location and each run is saved with a map of where you went. Deny it and the run still works — you just won't get the route.

Quick Start

  1. 1 Want to run right now without a plan? On the watch, use Quick Start and pick a bundled workout — an easy run, a long run, a tempo, intervals, or a fartlek.
  2. 2 These workouts guide effort in plain words and don't put a pace target on you, so they suit any runner. It's the fastest way to try Pace Buddy with no setup.
🗣️

Voice Coaching

  1. 1 Pace Buddy speaks the run to you so you can keep your eyes up: each step change, a heads-up before the next step, the halfway point, and each kilometre or mile split with its pace.
  2. 2 On a pace-target step it nudges you when you drift — "Speed up" or "Ease off" — and tells you when you're back on pace. It settles down as the run goes on so it coaches rather than chatters.
  3. 3 Cues duck your music or podcast rather than stopping it, and play over the watch speaker, AirPods, or any Bluetooth headphones.
  4. 4 Short countdown beeps and a haptic tap mark each step change, so you feel a transition even with the audio off. You can turn the beeps off in Settings.
📥

Importing a Plan

  1. 1 Already have a plan from a coach, a book, or a website? In New Plan, choose to read in an existing plan by pasting the text, or picking a file, PDF, or photo.
  2. 2 Pace Buddy reads it into a structured plan for you using Apple's on-device and Private Cloud Compute models — no third-party service. The plan text isn't kept after the import.
  3. 3 Every import is shown for review before it's saved, with anything uncertain flagged, so you can check the weeks and workouts against your source and fix anything before it goes into your plan.
💡 Importing needs a network connection. Once the plan is in, running it works completely offline like any other plan.
⏱️

Your 5K Time & Paces

  1. 1 Pace targets in a plan are worked out from your recent 5K time. You can type it in, pick a recent 5k from Health, or run the built-in 5K Time Trial on the watch to measure it.
  2. 2 When you run a faster 5k, Pace Buddy offers to re-work the paces in your current plan so the rest of your training keeps pace with you. Days you've already run are left as they were.
☁️

Progress & Sync

  1. 1 Finish a run and the day is marked done — on the watch and, when they're back in range, on the phone. Your progress toward the goal updates on the Today screen.
  2. 2 Tap a completed day to see the run's route, distance, pace, heart rate, and — where available — the weather at the time, credited to Apple Weather.
  3. 3 Your plan and its progress live in your own iCloud, so they stay in step across your devices. Finish a run out of range of your phone and it catches up as soon as the two are together again.
🔔

Reminders & Complication

  1. 1 Pace Buddy can remind you on a training day and stays quiet on a rest day. Add the complication to your watch face to see today's run at a glance and tap straight into it.
  2. 2 Today's run can also show up in the watch Smart Stack around the time you usually run, and you can ask Siri for today's run.
⚙️

Settings

  1. 1 Choose your units — kilometres or miles, or follow your system setting. The choice syncs across your devices.
  2. 2 Turn the countdown beeps on or off. Haptic taps at each step change stay on regardless, so you always feel a transition.
  3. 3 Set or update your 5K time, which is what your pace targets are worked out from.
